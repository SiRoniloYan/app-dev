## **Overview**
**Breaking Bad** is an American *crime drama* television series created by **Vince Gilligan**.  
It tells the story of **Walter White**, a chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing methamphetamine after being diagnosed with terminal cancer.

---

### **Main Characters**
- **Walter White** – A chemistry teacher turned meth producer.  
- **Jesse Pinkman** – Walter’s former student and business partner.  
- **Skyler White** – Walter’s wife.  
- **Hank Schrader** – DEA agent and Walter’s brother-in-law.  
- **Saul Goodman** – Criminal lawyer with a colorful personality.  

---

### **Why I Like It**
1. **Strong character development** – You literally see Walter White evolve from a humble teacher to a criminal mastermind.  
2. **Cinematic quality** – Every episode feels like a mini movie.  
3. **Moral complexity** – It makes you question what’s right or wrong.  
4. **Unforgettable quotes** –  
   - *“Say my name.”*  
   - *“I did it for me. I liked it. I was good at it.”*
